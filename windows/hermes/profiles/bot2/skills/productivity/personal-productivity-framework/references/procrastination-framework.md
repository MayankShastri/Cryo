# Procrastination Framework: Dual-Trigger Model

## 1. Trigger Breakdown

### Type A: Boredom & Under-stimulation (The "Too Easy" Trap)
- **Root Cause**: The required effort to achieve top results is minimal, leaving a large cognitive vacuum.
- **Symptom**: Procrastinating until the last minute because the lack of challenge breeds apathy. Relying on deadline panic to provide the missing focus/stimulation.
- **Countermeasure**:
  - Minimum Viable Effort: Cap time spent on low-challenge tasks.
  - Channel excess bandwidth into high-stimulus, self-directed projects.

### Type B: Friction & Identity Shock (The "Too Hard" Trap)
- **Root Cause**: Reaching a topic that does not "click" immediately. Encountering friction challenges the user's implicit self-concept of effortless mastery.
- **Symptom**: Feeling overwhelmed by ambiguity, not knowing the next 3 steps, and abandoning the task for cheap dopamine (social media, roleplay bots).
- **Countermeasure**:
  - **Micro-Stepping**: Reduce activation energy by making the first step stupidly small.
  - **Struggle Sprints**: 25-30 minute time-boxed commitment. Goal is remaining engaged, not solving the problem.
  - **Reframe Confusion**: View the feeling of being lost as the literal feeling of real learning occurring.

### Type C: Environmental Friction & Autonomy Defense (The "Surveillance" Trap)
- **Root Cause**: Intrusive, micro-managing, or non-supportive living environments where non-academic work (creative art, personal writing) triggers unsolicited judgment or interruptions.
- **Symptom**: Unconsciously delaying deep work until 1:00 AM – 4:00 AM because late night is the only guaranteed window of silence and autonomy; resulting in severe daytime sleepiness and fragmented afternoon nap cycles.
- **Countermeasure**:
  - **Stealth Scheduling**: Utilize the "Coding Shield"—running code or terminal tools on laptop during monitored family hours (e.g. 11:00 PM – 12:00 AM) where it looks like legitimate academic study and is left alone.
  - **Noise Storm Deflection**: Relocate or shift to passive tasks (eating, hygiene, audiobooks) during peak disruption hours (e.g. 7:00 PM).
  - **Sleep Consolidation**: Cap daytime naps at 30–90 mins to preserve natural sleep pressure for an earlier bedtime (~12:00 AM – 1:00 AM).
