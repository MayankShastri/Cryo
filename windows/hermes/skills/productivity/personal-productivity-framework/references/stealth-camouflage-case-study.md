# Case Study: Stealth Scheduling & PC Camouflage

This case study documents a real-world application of the Stealth Scheduling and Coding Shield principles for a high-capability student (Hunter / TheHunter171) living in an intrusive, highly-monitored household.

## 1. The PC Camouflage vs. Physical Artifact Trap
* **The Camouflage (Digital Screen):** Non-technical family members are completely oblivious to what is on a computer screen. A screen showing an IDE, terminal window, obscure code, or developer documentation is automatically categorized as *"he is studying/working."* It creates a perfect barrier of social invisibility.
* **The Trigger (Physical Artifacts):** The moment any physical artifact is introduced—such as a drawing tablet, a physical sketchbook, or a pen-and-paper journal—the camouflage is instantly shattered. These physical objects are highly legible to parents, triggering intrusive questions (*"What are you drawing?", "Why aren't you studying?", "Draw our portraits"*).
* **The Solution:** Keep 100% of the active evening workspace strictly on the PC screen (coding, technical reading, Web dev, Rust/Svelte). Keep physical journals, sketching, and private writing strictly locked within a late-night "Guaranteed Privacy" window when family members are asleep and surveillance falls to zero.

## 2. The Noise Storm & Spatial Relocation
* **The Conflict:** Daily at 7:00 PM, family arrival hijacks the primary working room with loud TV/Facebook phone speakers and constant interruptions. Trying to force deep, frustrating work (like Java DSA debugging) during this storm leads to severe cognitive load, irritability, and immediate doomscrolling.
* **The Solution (Displacement Shield):** Treat the noise storm (7:00 PM – 8:30 PM) as a low-intensity, low-cognitive-load zone. Relocate to a secondary quiet bed/room with headphones. Use this window for:
  - Having dinner or showering.
  - Consuming passive content (tech docs, video tutorials, audiobooks).
  - Guilt-free leisure (so that you don't waste your high-energy blocks on doomscrolling).

## 3. Reclaiming Autonomy from Late-Night Escapism
* **The Trap:** Staying up until 3:00 AM – 4:30 AM is frequently not a failure of discipline, but a coping mechanism to acquire quiet, uninterrupted personal space. However, this causes severe daytime fatigue, prompting massive 3-hour afternoon napped states, creating a vicious cycle.
* **The Solution:** 
  1. Cap afternoon napping to a strict **30–45 minute power nap** (or a 90-minute sleep cycle max) to build sleep pressure for the night.
  2. Shift the "Gold" deep-work window to **8:30 PM – 11:00 PM** under PC Camouflage.
  3. Schedule a peaceful **30-minute midnight sanctuary (12:00 AM – 12:30 AM)** for physical pen-and-paper journaling after parents fall asleep.
  4. Pass out by 12:30 AM for a consolidated, healthy 7-hour sleep cycle before university.
