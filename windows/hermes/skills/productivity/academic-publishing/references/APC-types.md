# Academic APC and Publishing Models

Understanding journal fee structures is critical to avoid surprise Article Processing Charges (APCs).

## Publishing Models

1. **Diamond Open Access (True 0-APC):**
   - Funded by universities, scientific societies, or government grants.
   - 100% free to read (Open Access) and 100% free to publish (Zero APC).
   - Examples: University press journals (*Periodica Polytechnica*, *AGH Computer Science*).

2. **Traditional / Hybrid Subscription (0-APC Option):**
   - Major publishers (Elsevier, Springer Nature, IEEE, Taylor & Francis, Wiley).
   - The journal offers two tracks upon acceptance:
     - **Open Access (Gold OA):** Author pays an APC (often $1,500–$4,000) for public access.
     - **Subscription / Standard Track:** $0 Author Fee. Readers/libraries pay subscription.
   - Always choose the **Subscription track** for zero-cost publication with full Scopus/SCI indexing.

3. **Gold Open Access (Paid APC):**
   - Entirely author-funded open access journals (e.g., MDPI, Frontiers, standalone OA publishers).
   - High risk of hidden or updated post-acceptance APCs even if listed as free in older spreadsheets.

## Verification Checklist
- [ ] Inspect official "Author Guidelines" / "Publishing Fees" webpage directly.
- [ ] Verify Scopus / Web of Science coverage on official master journal lists.
- [ ] Confirm submission template format (.docx / LaTeX / IEEE / ACM / Harvard).
